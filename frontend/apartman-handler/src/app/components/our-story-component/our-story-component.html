Our Story

Árnyas Apartment is more than just a place to stay
—it is a family story that has been growing for generations in Káptalanfüred,
on the northern shore of Lake Balaton.

The name Árnyas comes from Árnyas Street, the peaceful,
tree-lined road where the house stands.
Surrounded by nature and shaded by mature trees,
the area has always offered a sense of tranquility that inspired the identity
of our home.
The story began in the 1970s,
when our grandfather built the foundations of the house.
What was once a modest family holiday home gradually
became a place where generations gathered,
shared memories, and enjoyed summers by the lake.
Over the years, the building evolved together with our family.
Today, the house has been carefully renovated and transformed into a modern
apartment that combines contemporary comfort with its original character.
While much has changed since its early days,
the values on which it was built remain the same: family, connection,
and respect for the surrounding natural environment.
We believe that the beauty of Lake Balaton is found not only in its waters and landscapes,
but also in the moments spent together.
Árnyas Apartment reflects this harmony between family life and nature
—a place shaped by the past, renewed for the present,
and shared with those who appreciate a slower,
more meaningful way of experiencing the Balaton region.
